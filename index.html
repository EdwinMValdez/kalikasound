About

KALIKASOUND is an independent soundmapping project documenting the environmental and social impacts of development projects across the Philippines.

Through sound recordings and geographical mapping, the project captures the changing soundscapes of places affected by activities such as reclamation, mining, infrastructure expansion, and environmental degradation.

It explores sound as a means of documenting environmental change, understanding the relationship between people and their surroundings, and amplifying the often-overlooked experiences of affected communities.

What does development sound like, and whose voices are being drowned out?
